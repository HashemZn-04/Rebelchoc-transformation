With humour we rebel.
With humour we resist the lies.

The re-emergence of Manneken Pis as the symbol of defiance, humor and world resistance is to rise human consciousness against the potential destruction of our planet, through uncontrolled climate change and loss of human solidarity in solving political and human crisis.

We reject the destruction. We react to support the international and multilateral organisations that promote peace, solidarity, and problem-solving through negotiation.
