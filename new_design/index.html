Chapter I · 1621

The most renowned symbol of rebellion

Since the 14th century, Manneken Pis has stood in the heart of Brussels as the world's most renowned art symbol of defiance — rebellion, resistance, rejection, and satirical humour.

A small bronze boy with an irreverent answer to every empire that ever marched past him.

Chapter II · 1938

He never fails against blindfolded authorities

In 1938, as Nazism rose across Europe, Belgian artisans answered in bronze: Manneken Pis cast above the face of tyranny. Resistance with humour — until total victory.

Chapter III · 2026

The re-emergence

The re-emergence of Manneken Pis as the symbol of defiance, humour and world resistance has one purpose: to raise human consciousness against the accelerating self-destruction of our planet.

This time he is cast in Belgian chocolate — and printed on cotton.

With humour we rebel. With humour we resist the lies. We reject the destruction. We react — to support the international and multilateral organisations that promote peace, solidarity, and problem-solving through negotiation.

— The Rebel Choc manifesto
